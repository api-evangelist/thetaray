# ThetaRay

> ThetaRay is a financial-crime-compliance software company applying patented "Cognitive AI"
> anomaly detection to AML transaction monitoring, sanctions and customer screening, PEP screening
> and customer risk assessment for banks, fintechs and payment service providers. Its cloud-native
> SaaS platform analyzes SWIFT and domestic payment traffic together with KYC and risk data, and its
> RAY product runs agentic AI investigations over the resulting alerts inside a single case manager.

It is not published by ThetaRay. ThetaRay serves no /llms.txt of its own (404 on thetaray.com and
api.thetaray.com).

## Access status

- The ThetaRay API developer portal is at https://api.thetaray.com/openapi and is hosted on Redocly
  Cloud. Every route redirects (302) to an OIDC login at https://auth.cloud.redocly.com — a customer
  account is required to read the API reference or any OpenAPI document.
- The documentation host http://docs.thetaray.com/ redirects (302) to a Microsoft Entra SAML sign-in
  at login.microsoftonline.com.
- No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, A2A agent card, security.txt, status page, changelog, SDK or
  package is publicly retrievable. Access is arranged through sales, not self-serve signup.
