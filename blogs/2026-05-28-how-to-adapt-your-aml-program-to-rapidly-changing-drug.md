The risk landscape around drug trafficking isn’t just evolving, it’s accelerating. With illicit fentanyl now treated as a national security threat and new cartel-related designations emerging across Latin America, financial crime risk can shift overnight. Recent industry discussions, including ACAMS-led conversations have highlighted a growing challenge for AML teams.
